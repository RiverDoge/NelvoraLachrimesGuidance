By far the largest and most organized group that follows the Reverent Heart. Beyond their constant drive to better themselves and bring forth their leader, they generally try to avoid causing any strife. While their methods are unusual, their power is clear. Due to their diligence, and the presence of Lachrimé, most other groups tend to avoid them, or work diplomatically alongside their efforts.
